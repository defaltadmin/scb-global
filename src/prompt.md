- **OPTO T (Teams Voice):** global PSTN connectivity, cloud PBX replacement, hunt groups, IVR, emergency routing, compliant call recording. Delivered via Microsoft Operator Connect and Direct Routing.
- **OPTO C (Contact Center):** Azure-native omnichannel contact centre (voice, SMS, WhatsApp, email, AI sentiment analysis), built on Azure Communication Services and integrated with Anywhere365.
- **OPTO A (Attendant Console):** virtual receptionist and operator console inside Teams (presence, call screening, transfer).
- **OPTO I (Analytics):** real-time wallboards, historical analytics, call quality metrics, cost management, via Power BI and Azure telemetry.
- **OPTO X (CPaaS core):** multi-tenant, multi-carrier backbone with Class-4 switching and flow-builder routing, hosted on Microsoft Azure.
- **OPTO SHIELD / OPTO4Edge:** SD-WAN, SASE overlay and network security.

**Business model.** Hybrid SaaS plus PSTN: per-user monthly subscriptions, monthly PSTN trunking and calling bundles, SIP channel fees, number management, and migration professional services. Sold through direct sales plus channel agents, partners and system integrators.

**Market context.** Microsoft Teams Phone was reported at roughly 26 million PSTN users by early 2026, a small share (roughly 6 to 7 percent) of Teams' 350M+ monthly active users, which implies large whitespace for PSTN adoption.

**Saudi Arabia: how Teams voice actually works there.**
- The regulator is the Communications, Space and Technology Commission (CST).
- Native Microsoft Calling Plans are not available in KSA.
- Public voice termination must route through licensed local carriers (stc, Mobily, Zain/Salam).
- Virtual voice services reportedly require a CST permit (Virtual Voice Services Permit).
- PDPL (enforced by SDAIA) and NCA controls drive data-localisation requirements for personal data and call metadata.
- The go-to-market pattern for SCB in KSA is Direct Routing via local-carrier SIP trunks anchored to Azure Session Border Controllers, supported by regional channel agents and system integrators.
